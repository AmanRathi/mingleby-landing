# Mingleby — Claude Design Brief

Oct 9, 2026 · @Aman

## Overview

This brief gives Claude Design everything it needs to produce two things for mingleby.com: 10 minimal logo concepts and a bold, playful, mobile-first landing page that collects sign-ups in two steps.

**What Mingleby is:** a community where couples meet other couples in small groups over shared activities — dinners, hikes, board games, pottery, weekend getaways. Strictly platonic: no dating, no swinging, no weird vibes.

**What the page must do:**

1. Make the idea click in under 5 seconds (video hero + punchline).
2. Show the value: real activities, real couples, zero awkwardness.
3. Capture name + email first, then interests — so a drop-off still leaves a lead.
4. Feel fun and memorable: bold layout, small animations, a few hidden easter eggs.

**How to use this brief:** run it in two separate Claude Design sessions — logos first, then the landing page (so the chosen logo can go on the page). Attach the reference logo image to the logo session. Ready-to-paste prompts are in the "Prompts for Claude Design" section near the end; the sections in between are the detail those prompts point to.

## Brand foundations

The brand is a warm, cheeky friend who throws great get-togethers — bold yellow energy with a fuchsia punch.

**Colour palette** (starting values; Claude Design may tune shades but must keep yellow as the hero colour):

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Sunshine Yellow | #FFD23F | Backgrounds of big sections, highlights, logo |
| Secondary | Hot Fuchsia | #E6007E | CTAs, links, accents, hover states |
| Secondary alt | Cherry Red | #FF3B5C | Small accents, stickers, easter eggs |
| Ink | Night Plum | #1C1426 | Text, outlines, dark sections |
| Base | Cream | #FFF8EC | Page background, cards |

Rules: CTAs are always fuchsia on yellow/cream or yellow on plum. Text on yellow is always Night Plum (white on yellow fails contrast). Avoid generic gradients — use flat, confident colour blocks.

**Typography — sans-serif only, no serif anywhere (including the logo):**

- Display / headlines: **Bricolage Grotesque** (Google Fonts), 700–800 weight, tight letter-spacing, big sizes (56–120px desktop, 40–56px mobile).
- Body / UI: **DM Sans** (Google Fonts), 400–600 weight, 16–18px.
- No script fonts in the page UI; handwritten-style doodles may appear only as SVG illustrations.

**Voice and tone:** casual, conversational, a little cheeky — like a friend texting you. Short sentences, "you two" and "your crew", contractions, light humour. Never corporate ("curated sanctuary", "vetted access") and never cringey or suggestive. Every line should pass the test: would a friend say this out loud?

**Personality words:** warm, bold, playful, trustworthy, unpretentious.

**Visual motifs:** pairs of things (two cups, two circles, two dots), clinking/cheers moments, sticker-style badges, chunky outlines (2–3px plum strokes), slightly rotated cards, hand-drawn squiggle underlines.

## Landing page: sections and copy

Nine sections, top to bottom. The copy below is your Wix content rewritten in a casual voice — Claude Design should use it as-is and only trim for layout.

- Logo (left), links: How it works · Things we do · Our story · FAQ
- Fuchsia pill button: **Count us in**
- Mobile: logo + button + hamburger; menu opens as a full-screen yellow panel.

### 1. Hero (first fold) — video background

- Full-viewport muted, looping, autoplay video of couples laughing at a dinner table, hiking, playing board games. Dark plum overlay at \~40% so text reads.
- Small sticker badge above the headline: **No dating. No swiping. Just good company.**
- Headline: **Your couple friends are out there. Let's go find them.**
- Sub-line: Join small group hangouts with other couples — dinners, hikes, game nights and more. Show up as a pair, leave with a crew.
- Primary CTA (fuchsia, big): **Register now** — opens the 2-step signup.
- Secondary link: See what we get up to ↓
- Alternative headlines to test: "Double dates, but make it a party." · "Two's company. Six is a weekend plan." · "Find your couple crew."

### 2. The problem

- Heading: **Making couple friends as adults? Weirdly hard.**
- Three tilted sticker cards:
  - **New city, no crew.** You love your life together — but weekends feel a bit… quiet.
  - **Dating apps? Not for you.** You're already taken, thanks. And "lifestyle" apps bring the wrong vibes.
  - **The 4-way click is rare.** Finding a couple where all four of you actually get along usually happens by pure luck.
- Punchline under the cards: **Mingleby takes the luck out of it.**

### 3. How it works

- Heading: **Four steps. Zero awkward small talk.**
  1. **Sign up together** — Tell us who you are and what you two are into. Takes a minute.
  2. **Get the green light** — We do a quick check on every couple (24–48 hrs) so everyone's who they say they are.
  3. **Build your duo profile** — Your neighbourhood, a couple of pics, your top passions.
  4. **Show up and hang** — RSVP to a dinner, a hike or a game night. Bring your appetite and your partner.

## Two-step signup flow

Step 1 saves name and email to your backend the moment it's submitted; step 2 adds interests to the same record, so an abandoned step 2 still leaves you a lead.

Opens as a modal (desktop) or full-screen sheet (mobile) from every "Register now" / "Count us in" button. The final CTA section uses step 1 inline.

**Step 1 of 2 — "First things first"**

- Heading: **Hey there, you two!** Sub: Drop your details and you're on the list.
- Fields: Your name · Partner's name (optional) · Email
- Button: **Save our spot →**
- On submit: `POST /api/signup` with `{name, partner_name, email, source, utm_*}` → backend returns a `lead_id`. Show the button loading state; on success, slide to step 2. On error, a friendly inline message: "Oops, that didn't go through. Try again?"
- Validation: email format check, inline (not alerts).

**Step 2 of 2 — "What are you two into?"**

- Heading: **Nice — you're in! Now the fun part.** Sub: Pick everything you'd both enjoy. We'll match you to the right hangouts.
- Interests as big tappable chips (multi-select) using the 11 activity names from section 4, each with an emoji. Selected chip turns fuchsia with a little bounce.
- Optional: City (text or dropdown) — helps prioritise launch cities.
- Button: **Done — we're ready to mingle!** · Text link: Skip for now
- On submit: `PATCH /api/signup/{lead_id}` with `{interests[], city}`.

**Success screen**

- Confetti burst in yellow + fuchsia, two cups clinking animation.
- Copy: **Cheers! 🥂 You're on the list.** We'll email you as soon as hangouts open near you. Psst — couples who share Mingleby with friends get in first.
- Share buttons: WhatsApp, Instagram, copy link.

**Design notes:** a progress pill ("1 of 2" → "2 of 2") at the top; step 1 must fit above the keyboard on a small phone; remember entered values if the modal is closed and reopened.

## Animations and easter eggs

Keep motion small and snappy (150–400ms), and switch it all off for people who set reduced motion on their device.

**Everyday animations:**

- Hero headline words pop in one by one; the "No dating" sticker wobbles gently.
- Section headings get a hand-drawn squiggle underline that draws itself on scroll.
- Activity cards tilt slightly and lift on hover; on mobile, they snap-scroll.
- CTA buttons have a springy press and a tiny shine sweep every few seconds.
- "How it works" steps light up one after another as you scroll.
- A cursor-follow pair of dots on desktop (two little circles that trail the pointer together).

**Easter eggs (pick 3–4):**

1. **Clink the logo** — click/tap the logo cups 3 times and they clink, spill a few coffee drops and a "Cheers!" bubble appears.
2. **Konami code** — typing ↑↑↓↓←→←→ B A rains tiny cups and confetti across the page.
3. **Pair up** — the activity cards secretly come in pairs; hovering one makes its "partner" card wave.
4. **Footer duo** — two small characters in the footer high-five when you scroll to the very bottom.
5. **Lonely dot** — a single dot floats in the hero; drag it next to another dot and they bounce happily ("See? Better together.").
6. **Tab title** — when the visitor switches tabs, the page title changes to "Come back, your crew misses you 🥺".

## Responsive and technical requirements

Design mobile-first: most visitors will arrive from WhatsApp and Instagram on a phone.

| Breakpoint | Width | Key changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mobile | 360–767px | Single column, hamburger nav, activity carousel, signup as full-screen sheet, 16px side padding |
| Tablet | 768–1023px | 2-column cards, nav condensed |
| Desktop | 1024px+ | Full nav, 3–4 column activity grid, cursor effects, signup modal |

**Hero video:**

- MP4 (H.264) + WebM, under 4MB, 10–15 seconds, no audio, `muted autoplay loop playsinline`.
- A poster image shows instantly while the video loads; on slow connections or data-saver mode, show the poster only.
- Use a separate, lighter vertical crop for mobile.
- Sources: stock (Pexels, Coverr) for launch; replace with real Mingleby footage later.

**Performance and accessibility:**

- Load under 3 seconds on 4G; images in WebP, lazy-loaded below the fold.
- Text contrast meets WCAG AA; tap targets at least 44px.
- All animations respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Form fields have labels (not placeholder-only) and clear error messages.

**Tracking:** pass UTM parameters into the signup payload; fire events for `hero_cta_click`, `step1_submit`, `step2_submit`, `step2_skip`, `share_click`.
